How we work out these numbers
  • Listings show asking prices; auction results show prices paid. Prices are for just the arm, without the tool on its end or the work to set it up.
  • We have no auction result for this robot yet, so the estimate leans on sales of other arms. Across all arms, the middle auction sale brought 24% of the typical asking price.
