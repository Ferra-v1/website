Our estimate for a planned private sale of a 6 year old one, resting on 3 listings, where the owner has time to find a buyer. Every other price on this page is what sellers ask.

Under 10 years old we go by the 6 younger arms sold at auction, which brought about 50% of the ask, or about 28% if we count one repeated robot once.
