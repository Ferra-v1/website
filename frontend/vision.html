You may have walked past one already.

Robots will do more and more of the world's work.

Robots in real buildings are hard to copy.

Someone had to find the building, bolt the robot to the floor, teach it the job and keep it running. Anyone else who wants that work has to do all of it again.

We think that head start lasts. It is why these robots are worth owning.

When a robot does the work, the money goes to whoever owns it.

Right now, most people have no way to own any of them.

First, a fair price.

$14,700is what sellers typically ask for a 10 year old robot arm.

$6,100is our estimate of what it would likely sell for.

We read what sellers ask and check what auctions pay, so anyone can look it up. This part is here today.

In tests our asking prices miss by about 25%, and we have no test against real sales yet. How we test them →

Next, loans.

Once people can check a price, lenders can lend money for robots, as they already do for trucks and planes.

In our example, a loan pays for 70% of a robot. The bright dots show that share.

Lenders provide the money, and we arrange it. We are working on this now, and we have not arranged a loan yet.

One day, a piece for anyone.

We want anyone, one day, to be able to own a piece of the robots doing the work.

You cannot invest through Ferra today. This is where we want to go.

Help us get there.
