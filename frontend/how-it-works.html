How a company comes to own its robots.

Follow one example loan, from the first question to the last payment. You cannot invest through Ferra today.

The five steps

  1. An example company wants 50 new robot arms at $125,000 each, $6,250,000 in all. Ferra would check the price and suggest a loan of 70%: $4,375,000.

  2. We plan to take those numbers to lenders, and each would decide for itself. Ferra has not arranged any loans yet.

  3. The company would get $4,375,000, add $1,875,000 of its own and buy the robots. It would pay Ferra a one-time fee of about $65,625.

  4. The robots work for customers, who would pay rent. In our example that is $4,500 a robot, or $225,000 a month.

  5. From that rent it would pay about $121,500 a month, plus interest. After month 36 nothing is owed, and the company owns all 50 robots.
