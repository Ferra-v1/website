Used KUKA KR 16 prices by age

A KUKA KR 16 built in 2016 typically lists for $16,900 for the arm alone, and most sellers ask between $10,000 and $28,500.

In a planned private sale it would likely bring about $7,000. It is part of the KUKA KR family.

At 10 years old the KR 16 asks 15% more than the KUKA KR family as a whole ($14,700). Its asking price falls about 4.1% a year. What it would sell for drops more sharply at 10 years old, because arms that old brought a middle 17% of the asking price at auction, against 50% for younger arms.

Some versions have enough listings for a price of their own: the KR 16 L6 asks $17,400 at 10 years old.

How we worked it out

One model fitted across every used arm listing gives how asking prices fall with age. The KR 16 has enough listings of its own for its own adjustment to the family level, and the band is where 8 in 10 asking prices for this model and age would fall. The expected sale sits halfway between the asking price and the auction floor on a ratio scale. Every step is on how we price robots.
