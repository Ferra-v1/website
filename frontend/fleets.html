How we work out these numbers
  • Example: 50 new arms at $125,000 each, with 70% lent. Real terms depend on the lender and the robots. Nothing here is an offer to lend.
  • The rent is our example, the one our price tool opens on. Every robot is rented all month, and interest is left out because the lender sets it.
  • Our price tool does the fuller sum with interest. It shows a smaller loan, about 65% for a new factory arm at this rent, because the rent has to cover the payment with room to spare.
