Questions

Is Ferra a lender?

No. Ferra prices the robots and arranges the loan. Lenders provide the money.

Who provides the money?

Investment funds that lend money against equipment.

Which robots qualify?

Robots that people buy and sell used, so we can see what they are worth.

How much can we borrow?

In our example, 70% of the fleet's value. The share depends on the robot and the lender.

What does it cost?

In our example, a fee of about 1.5% when the loan starts, plus the lender's interest.

What happens if a robot company fails?

We aim to keep the loan smaller than what the robots could be sold for, so they can be sold to pay back what they can.
