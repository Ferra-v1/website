Questions

Has Ferra arranged a loan yet?

Not yet. Ferra is new, and the loans on this site are examples.

Does Ferra lend the money?

No. Ferra prices the robots and arranges the loan. Lenders provide the money.

Who provides the money?

We plan to work with investment funds that lend money for machines. None has lent through us yet.

How much can I borrow?

In our example, 70% of what new robots cost. For a used robot, 70% of our estimate of what it is worth, set up and working. A real lender may count less and offer less.

What does it cost?

In our example, a fee of about 1.5% when the loan starts, plus the lender's interest.

How do I pay it back?

In our example, in equal monthly parts over 36 months. The rent your robots earn is what pays it.

What if a robot company fails?

A lender can sell the robots if a company fails. A forced sale may not cover the loan, so we show lenders the sums.
