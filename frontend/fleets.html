Why a loan

Raising equity for each new robot gives away a slice of the company every time the fleet grows. A fleet loan is repaid from the rent the robots earn.

Keep your equity

Selling shares to buy robots hands part of the company to investors each time the fleet grows. A loan pays for the robots and your ownership stays whole.
