How much to trust this.

We found 84 listings. 44 show a price. 40 also show a year. 38 pass our checks.

38 listings from 16 sellers pass our checks. We leave out listings that repeat one robot, sell many at once, or are new or broken. That is enough for this robot to have its own price level.

We tested this. We hid each seller's listings and guessed their prices from everyone else's. Our typical miss on this robot was 37%.

Asking prices fall about 4% a year across all robot arms. This robot uses that same rate, because its own listings do not show a clearly different one.
