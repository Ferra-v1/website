These are two dots on the chart above, two arms built a year apart. We picked the sale in the middle of the 7 we have for this robot, so it is no extreme. It sold in 2022, and we do not adjust old sales for inflation.

A planned private sale likely lands between the ask and these results. We estimate $3,800 to $15,100 for a 13 year old one, resting on 2 listings.

Our rule changes at 10 years old: older arms brought far less at auction, so the estimate drops in one step.
