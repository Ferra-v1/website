Privacy

We keep what you type into a form on this site, and we never sell it.

What we collect

What you type into a form on this site: your work email and, depending on the form, your company or firm, your role, robot type, number of robots, price per robot, funding timeline, typical deal size, or your message. We also record which page the form was on and any campaign tags in the link you followed.

Why

To answer you, price your robots, send rough loan terms, or share our study and sample terms. We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are stored in a database hosted by Supabase in the United States. The website can only add submissions; it cannot read them back. Only the Ferra team can see them.

Who we share it with

If you ask for financing, we may share your robot details with lenders who finance equipment, only to arrange your loan and only after we have spoken with you.

Analytics and cookies

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits. It does not use cookies or follow you across other websites. We do not use advertising cookies.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you sent us, write to us through the contact form and we will do it.
