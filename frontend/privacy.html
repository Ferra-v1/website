Who we are

Ferra is run by its founders. This policy covers every form on ferra.company. To reach us, use the form at the end of this page.

What we collect

What you type into a form on this site: your work email and, depending on the form:

  • your company or firm
  • your robot type, how many robots you have and the price per robot
  • when you need the money
  • your message

We also record which page the form was on and, if you came from an ad or email link, which one.

The price tool runs in your browser. What you type into it stays on your device.

Why

To answer you, price your robots, send a rough idea of the loan, or share our study and sample terms. We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

In a database hosted by Supabase in the United States. The website can only add to it, and only the Ferra team can read it. A copy of each form is also emailed to the founders through FormSubmit.

Who we share it with

If you ask about a loan, we may share your robot details with lenders who finance equipment. We do this only to set up your loan, and only after we have spoken with you.

How long we keep it

We keep what you send us for 2 years, then delete it. If you ask us to delete it sooner, we will.

Analytics and cookies

We count page visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not follow you across other websites. We use no advertising cookies.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you sent us, write to us through the form at the end of this page and we will do it.
