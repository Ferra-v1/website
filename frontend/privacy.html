What Ferra collects through this website and what we do with it.

01What we collect

Only what you type into a form on this site: your work email and, depending on the form, your company or firm, your role, robot type, number of robots, price per robot, funding timeline, typical deal size, or your message. We also record which page the form was on and any campaign tags in the link you followed.

02Why

To reply to you, price your fleet, send indicative terms, or share our study and sample terms. We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

03Where it is stored

Form submissions are stored in a database hosted by Supabase in the United States. The website can only add submissions; it cannot read them back. Only the Ferra team can see them.

04Who we share it with

If you ask for financing, we may share your fleet details with lenders who finance equipment, only to arrange your loan and only after we have spoken with you.

05Analytics and cookies

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits. It does not use cookies or follow you across other websites. We do not use advertising cookies.

06Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you sent us, write to us through the contact form and we will do it.
