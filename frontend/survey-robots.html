How robot companies pay for their fleets

Ferra is writing a free report on what robots are worth and how robot fleets get paid for. This survey takes about five minutes. We report answers only in groups of five or more companies, and we never name you or your company. Everyone who answers gets the report before it is public, plus a private comparison of their fleet against the Ferra Robot Index if they want one.
