Robot companies should be able to borrow against their fleets.

Robot companies rent their robots out. Every robot they deploy is paid for up front, and raising equity for each one is slow and expensive.

Lenders already finance trucks, aircraft and factory machines, because they know what those machines resell for. Robots have lacked that price history.

Ferra is building it. We price robots from used-robot listings and auction results, and use those prices to arrange loans from lenders who finance equipment.

That is the destination: every robot carries a price a lender can trust, and a founder can deploy more robots and keep the company.
