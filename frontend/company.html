Robots had no price list.
So we are writing one.

Ferra helps robot companies borrow the money to buy their robots. Every one of those loans starts with knowing what a robot is worth.

Robot companies rent their robots out, and the rent comes in a little each month. But they must pay for every robot up front, so the more customers they win, the more money they need before anyone has paid them.

Lenders already lend for trucks and planes, because they know what those sell for used. Robots have had no such record of prices.

So we are building one. We read what sellers ask for used robots and what auctions pay, we turn that into a fair price for each robot, and we use those prices to arrange loans with lenders.

We do not lend the money ourselves. Lenders do. Our job is to give everyone a number they can trust.

That is the first step. Where we want it to go is here.
