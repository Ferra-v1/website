Robots had no price list. So we are writing one.

Ferra helps robot companies borrow the money to buy their robots. Every one of those loans starts with knowing what a robot is worth.

We are Divy and Praj.We started Ferra.

Robot companies rent their robots out, and the rent comes in a little each month. But they must pay for every robot up front, so the more customers they win, the more money they need before anyone has paid them.

Lenders already lend for trucks and planes, because they know what those sell for used. We could not find a public record of used robot prices, so we are building one.

We read what sellers ask for used robots and what auctions pay, and we turn that into a fair price for each robot. We use those prices to arrange loans with lenders.

We do not lend the money ourselves. Lenders do. Our job is to give everyone a number they can check.

That is the first step. Where we want it to go is here.

What you will not find here.

  • Money from us.

    Ferra is not a lender. Lenders provide the money.

  • A way to invest.

    You cannot invest through Ferra today.

  • A long track record.

    Ferra has not arranged a loan yet, and the loans on this site are examples.

  • A price for every kind of robot.

    Our listings cover robot arms so far. For other robots we use our best estimate until more listings come in.
