Who gets to own the robots?

Robots will do more and more of the world's work. The ones already working in real buildings are hard to copy, and that makes them valuable.

Right now, they are hard even to buy. Robot companies rent their robots out, but they must pay for every robot up front.

Lenders already lend for trucks and planes, because they know what those sell for used. Robots have had no such record of prices.

So we are building one. We price robots from what sellers ask and what auctions pay, and we use those prices to arrange loans.

That is the first step. Once people trust the price of a robot, more people can put their money behind robots. One day, we want anyone to be able to own a piece of the robots doing the work.
