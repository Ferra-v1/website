Robots had no price list.
So we are writing one.

Ferra helps robot companies borrow the money to buy their robots. Every one of those loans starts with knowing what a robot is worth.

We are Divy and Praj.We started Ferra.

We could not find a public record of used robot prices, so we are building one.

It matters because robot companies rent their robots out, and the rent comes in a little each month. They must pay for every robot up front, so the more customers they win, the more money they need before anyone has paid them.

Lenders already lend for trucks and planes, because they know what those sell for used. Robots need that same number.

We read what sellers ask for used robots and what auctions pay, and we turn that into a fair price for each robot. We plan to use those prices to arrange loans with lenders. Our job is to give everyone a number they can check.

That is the first step. Where we want it to go is here.
