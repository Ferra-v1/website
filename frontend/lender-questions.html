Questions from lenders

What does a forced sale of robots bring?

Less than sellers ask. We matched 10 auction sales to listings of the same robot at the same age. The middle one brought 24% of the asking price, the lowest 15% and the highest 72%.

Do older robots sell for a smaller share?

Yes. Robots under 10 years old brought a middle 50% of what sellers ask (6 sales). Robots 10 years or older brought 17% (4 sales).

Where do the prices come from?

From 1,744 public listings of used robots and 26 auction results, gathered from 7 sources. Listings show asking prices. Auction results show prices paid.

How much would an example loan be?

On the price tool’s opening example, the loan shown is 65% of the price paid. With a named backup partner, who keeps the robots running if the robot company fails, it is 68%.

What happens if a robot company goes under?

Our model assumes a 9% chance each year that a robot company fails, and that someone keeps the robots running in 55% of those failures. A failure cuts what the robots are worth before any sale.

What would a lender receive?

Three documents: the terms, a price for every robot, and the study behind the prices.

Is Ferra a lender?

No. Ferra is not a lender. Nothing here is an offer to lend or to sell an investment.
