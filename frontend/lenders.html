Example terms

Equipment loan, robot fleet

Securitywhat backs the loan
50 installed robot arms and the rent contracts they work under. If the loan is not paid back, the lender can sell the robots and collect the rent.
Fleet Valuewhat the robots cost
$6,250,000, the price of each robot added up.
Advance Ratethe share lent
70% of Fleet Value.
Loanthe money lent
$4,375,000.
Repaymenthow it is paid back
Equal monthly amounts over 36 months from the day the loan starts, plus interest set by the Lender.
Feewhat Ferra is paid, once
About 1.5% of the Loan when it starts: $65,625.
Arrangerwho sets it up
Ferra, which prices the robots and will arrange the Loan.
Lenderwho provides the money
A fund that lends money for machines.

An example. Real terms depend on the lender and the robots. Not an offer or a promise to lend.
