FerraExample terms

Equipment loan, robot fleet

Securitywhat backs the loan
50 installed robot arms and the rent contracts they work under. If the loan is not paid back, the lender can sell the robots and collect the rent.
Fleet Valuewhat the robots cost
$6,250,000, the price of each robot added up.
Advance Ratethe share lent
70% of Fleet Value.
Loanthe money lent
$4,375,000.
Repaymenthow it is paid back
Equal monthly amounts over 36 months from the day the loan starts, plus interest set by the Lender.
Feewhat Ferra would be paid, once
About 1.5% of the Loan when it starts: $65,625.
Arrangerwho would set it up
Ferra, which would price the robots and arrange the Loan.
Lenderwho provides the money
A fund that lends money for machines.

How we work out these numbers
  • Every loan on this page is an example: 50 new arms with 70% lent. Real terms depend on the lender and the robots. Nothing here is an offer to lend.
  • Values by age come from our model of a robot fully set up and working. Loss figures come from a model in our 17-page study of 18 robot types.
  • The 10 sales are auction results we could match to public listings. Listings show asking prices. Auction results show prices paid.
