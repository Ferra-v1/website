Questions

Who provides the money?

Private credit lenders who finance equipment. Ferra prices the robots and arranges the loan.

Which robots qualify?

Robots with a real resale market, starting with the types in our study, such as industrial arms and mobile robots.

How much can we borrow?

In our example, 70% of the fleet's value. The advance depends on the robot type and the lender.

What does it cost?

In our example, a fee of about 1.5% at close plus the lender's interest rate.

Tell us about your fleet.

We price each robot and come back with indicative terms.
