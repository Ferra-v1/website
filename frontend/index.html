The place a robot holds is hard to take.

A robot already working in a place holds that place. Someone had to find the building, bolt it down, teach it the job and keep it running. Anyone else who wants that work has to do all of it again.

We think that head start lasts, and that is why these robots are worth owning.

What if a loan goes bad?

If a company fails, a lender can sell its robots. A forced sale may not cover the loan, so we plan to show lenders the sums first.
