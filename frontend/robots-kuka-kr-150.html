Used KUKA KR 150 prices by age

A KUKA KR 150 built in 2016 typically lists for $12,500 for the arm alone, and most sellers ask between $7,400 and $21,200.

In a planned private sale it would likely bring about $5,200. It is part of the KUKA KR family.

How we worked it out

One model fitted across every used arm listing gives how asking prices fall with age. The KR 150 has enough listings of its own for its own adjustment to the family level, and the band is where 8 in 10 asking prices for this model and age would fall. The expected sale sits halfway between the asking price and the auction floor on a ratio scale. Every step is on how we price robots.
