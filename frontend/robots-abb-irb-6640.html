Used ABB IRB 6640 prices by age

An ABB IRB 6640 built in 2010 typically lists for $8,900 for the arm alone, and most sellers ask between $5,300 and $15,000.

In a planned private sale it would likely bring about $3,700. It is part of the ABB IRB 6000 series family.

At 16 years old the IRB 6640 asks 7% less than the ABB IRB 6000 series family as a whole ($9,600). Its asking price falls about 4.1% a year. What it would sell for drops more sharply at 10 years old, because arms that old brought a middle 17% of the asking price at auction, against 50% for younger arms.

Some versions have enough listings for a price of their own: the IRB 6640-205/2.75 asks $7,100 at 16 years old.

How we worked it out

One model fitted across every used arm listing gives how asking prices fall with age. The IRB 6640 has enough listings of its own for its own adjustment to the family level, and the band is where 8 in 10 asking prices for this model and age would fall. The expected sale sits halfway between the asking price and the auction floor on a ratio scale. Every step is on how we price robots.
