How much to trust this.

We found 43 listings. 25 show a price. 16 also show a year. 14 pass our checks.

One dot for each listing we found. The darker the dot, the more of our checks it passed.

14 listings from 9 sellers pass our checks. We leave out listings that repeat one robot, sell many at once, or are new or broken. With this few, the price leans on what other arms ask, and the range is wide.

We have too few listings to test our guess on this robot alone. Across all arms, when we hid each seller's listings and guessed their prices, our typical miss was 25%.

Asking prices fall about 4% a year across all robot arms. This robot uses that same rate, because it has too few listings to show its own.
